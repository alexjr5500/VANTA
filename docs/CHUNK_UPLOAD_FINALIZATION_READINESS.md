# Chunk upload finalization readiness

Atomic ACTIVE -> FINALIZING claim prevents competing provider writes; metadata, queue work (when enabled), and exact COMPLETE file binding commit together. Legacy COMPLETE sessions without a verified identity fail closed rather than returning another file. Late cancellation/failure cannot downgrade finalizing or completed sessions. Parts are immutable after publication and assembled with stream backpressure. A provider or ambiguous metadata failure may leave FINALIZING and a retained asset for manual reconciliation; do not retry by resetting status or deleting originals. Existing local staging storage is not shared across app replicas; distributed uploads require storage/routing design and acceptance. No production changes. Migration is additive and requires explicit release approval. CI and real database/provider acceptance remain required.
